TIMELINEA typical outpatient visit

From booking to the pharmacy

  1. 1
    Before the visit

    Find the hospital’s official WeChat account

    Open its official account or mini-program and find appointment services.

  2. 2
    Before the visit

    Create a patient profile

    Add passport details online or verify them at the hospital.

  3. 3
    Booking

    Choose the campus, department, and date

    Save confirmation and arrive early.

  4. 4
    Arrival

    Check in and verify your identity

    Show your passport or appointment record.

  5. 5
    Consultation

    Wait for your number and see the doctor

    Explain your concern and show relevant records or medicines.

  6. 6
    Tests

    Pay for and complete examinations

    Payment usually comes first. Keep receipts and reports.

  7. 7
    Follow-up

    Collect results and return

    A new check-in may be required.

  8. 8
    Pharmacy

    Pay for and collect medicine

    Follow the collection instructions.
