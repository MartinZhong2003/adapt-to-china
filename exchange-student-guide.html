STUDENT ROUTE · GENERAL PLANNING GUIDE

Your exchange in China, step by step.

Prepare documents, arrival, a local number, registration, and any required health or residence steps.

Use this as a planning framework, not official immigration instructions.

Requirements vary. Confirm your category, deadlines, and documents with your university and official visa or immigration services.

BEFORE YOU FLYUniversity instructions → study documents → visa process → arrival plan
AFTER YOU LANDLocal SIM → campus and address registration → health check if required → residence document
PART 01

Before arriving

Build your plan around the university’s official sequence.

  1. 1
    UNIVERSITY FIRST

    Confirm dates and the official checklist

    Ask which documents, originals, scans, and deadlines apply.

    Another student’s route may not apply to you.
  2. 2
    VISA PREPARATION

    Apply for the specified study visa

    Use the official service for your residence and prepare the requested materials. From 15 September 2026, a new State Council regulation expressly requires entry, stay, and residence application purposes to be truthful and lawful, and authorities may request supporting documents, materials, or electronic data when verifying an application.

    Confirm the category before submitting and keep your application details consistent with your supporting records.
  3. 3
    DOCUMENT CONTROL

    Prepare the submission and keep receipts

    Print confirmations, check photos, bring requested originals, and copy the application.

    Store scans separately.
  4. 4
    TRAVEL TIMING

    Plan around registration and housing

    Check campus and dormitory dates. Confirm early or late arrival.

PART 02

After arriving

Later steps often depend on a local number and earlier records.

  1. 1
    PRACTICAL SETUP

    Get a Chinese SIM card

    Bring your passport and compare price, data, term, and cancellation.

    Local numbers unlock many university and daily-life systems.
  2. 2
    REGISTRATION

    Complete campus and address registration

    Follow university check-in and confirm your accommodation registration. Non-hotel registration is generally required within 24 hours; an official online pilot is available in Hebei, Liaoning, Zhejiang, Hubei, Guangxi, Chongqing, and Sichuan through NIA 12367 channels.

    Keep paper or digital proof and confirm the local route with your university.
  3. 3
    IF REQUIRED

    Complete the designated health check

    Use the facility and channel specified by your university.

    Confirm appointments, photos, fasting, and required reports.
  4. 4
    IMMIGRATION FOLLOW-UP

    Complete required residence steps

    Follow the university and immigration timetable for any conversion or extension.

    Use the stated deadline. For general enquiries, call 12367.
PART 03

During your exchange

Keep your status, address, insurance, travel plans, and university records aligned throughout the semester.

  1. 1
    STATUS AND ADDRESS

    Track expiry dates and changes

    Keep documents, registration, enrolment, and insurance current.

  2. 2
    TRAVEL

    Check re-entry before leaving

    Review entries and validity. Ask the university or 12367 if unclear.

  3. 3
    WORK OR INTERNSHIP

    Get approval first

    Confirm university approval and required document annotations.

  4. 4
    SUPPORT

    Use support early

    Save international office, clinic, counselling, and emergency contacts.

PART 04

Before leaving

Plan clearance, housing, refunds, and accounts early.

  1. 1
    UNIVERSITY CLEARANCE

    Confirm completion steps

    Ask when records, certificates, and clearances will be ready.

  2. 2
    HOUSING

    Document checkout

    Photograph the room, meters, keys, and handover. Confirm refunds.

  3. 3
    ACCOUNTS

    Choose what to close

    Review accounts and keep a recovery method that works abroad.

  4. 4
    RECORDS

    Store a departure folder

    Keep secure copies of status, insurance, academic, contract, and payment records.

DOCUMENT-WALLET RULE

Carry the paperwork that unlocks the next step.

During the first weeks, keep originals secure and copies easy to reach.

  • Passport and current visa or entry document
  • Admission and enrolment documents
  • JW202 or JW201, where applicable
  • Temporary Residence Registration
  • Medical certificate, if required
  • Passport photos and digital scans
  • Receipts and appointment confirmations
  • University contact details
