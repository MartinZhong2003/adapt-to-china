YOUR MOVING PATH

Four stages. One next step.

Formal steps depend on your status and host. Confirm them with the responsible authority.

01
1–3 MONTHS BEFORE ARRIVAL

Clarify the formal route and the support gap.

Ask your employer or host for a timeline. Separate their tasks from yours.

  • Confirm your arrival date and official contact.
  • Ask what accommodation is arranged and the backup plan.
  • Check phone compatibility, roaming, and SIM needs.
  • Confirm when health coverage starts and save an emergency contact.
Common failureAssuming “HR handles relocation” includes housing, phone, payments, or family needs.
BackupAsk for a named person and a written owner beside every task.
02
2–4 WEEKS BEFORE ARRIVAL

Prepare the day-one essentials.

Save essentials before data, foreign cards, or English search fail.

  • Keep your address in Chinese and English.
  • Save key contacts offline.
  • Prepare two payment routes and some emergency cash.
  • Keep copies and confirmations in one secure folder.
