Where to actually look.

Start with these platforms. Fees and coverage change, so confirm current details in-app.

Part two

What tenants learn the hard way.

Practical checks shared by recent tenants.

01

Before you start looking

Do not sign more than a year

Keep a first contract to one year or less; early exit can be expensive.

Agree the agent fee in writing

Before viewing, confirm the agent, payer, and exact fee in writing.

Text, do not talk

Use written WeChat messages for translation and a record of promises.

Be ready to receive calls and pay

Before time-sensitive viewings, confirm that your phone number works in China and that you have a payment method accepted by the listing platform or housing provider.

Write every non-negotiable down

Include allergies, smoke sensitivity, pets, sunlight, noise, minimum space, furniture, appliances, and shared-area concerns before the search begins.

02

At the viewing

Film everything, with the landlord in shot

Before move-in, record every room, mark, and damaged surface on video.

Run the water, then run it again

Test taps, shower pressure, drains, and toilets.

Test the wifi, do not take their word

Connect your own phone and test speed before signing.

Ask the utility rate, not the bill

Ask for electricity and water rates; commercial rates can cost more.

Photos are marketing

Judge the in-person viewing, not edited photos. Never deposit sight unseen.

Watch for a subdivided flat

Avoid suspicious subdivided rooms and verify the property’s lawful use.

Inspect beyond the room

Check hallways and shared areas for smoke, pet exposure, odors, noise, cleanliness, and anything relevant to your health or comfort.

Check light at a useful time

Photos cannot prove everyday sunlight. Visit when natural light matters to you, and check what nearby buildings block.

03

Before you sign

Check they own it

Match the owner’s ID, ownership certificate, and contract. For sublets, request written permission.

Nothing verbal counts

Put rent coverage, repairs, deposit return, and early-exit terms in the contract.

Confirm they will register you

Confirm the landlord will support required accommodation registration. See the paperwork guide.

Understand any holding payment first

Before paying to reserve a unit, get the exact room, amount, holding period, refund rules, and whether the money will count toward rent or the deposit in writing. Use an official payment channel and keep the receipt.

04

Once you live there

Report repairs in writing

Report repairs with photos. Confirm who arranges and pays before spending.

Buy a mattress topper

If the mattress is too firm, confirm its size before buying a topper.

Cover the surfaces you will ruin

Protect mattresses, counters, desks, and kitchen surfaces from stains.

Do not host someone who is out of status

Do not host someone with unlawful stay documents. Direct them to 12367 or the exit-entry authority.

The words

Six phrases that decide the deal.

Six common listing and contract terms.

押一付三
yā yī fù sān

One-month deposit plus three months’ rent. Ask whether monthly payment is available.

中介费
zhōngjiè fèi

Agent fee, paid on top of rent and deposit. Confirm and negotiate.

民水民电
mín shuǐ mín diàn

Residential utility rates. 商水商电 usually costs more.

整租 / 合租
zhěng zū / hé zū

Whole flat, rather than a room in a shared flat.

无中介费
wú zhōngjiè fèi

No agent fee. Still verify who posted the listing.

二房东
èr fáng dōng

A subletter. Ask for the owner’s written permission.
