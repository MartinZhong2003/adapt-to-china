Before you go

  • Bring the patient’s passport or accepted identity document and insurance information.
  • Save the exact Chinese hospital name and address.
  • Confirm the campus, department, building and entrance before departure.
  • Arrange language support in advance when it matters.
  • Confirm insurance, direct billing, deposits and preauthorization with both the hospital and insurer.
  • For a life-threatening emergency in China, call 120.
