Before you go

  • Bring your passport and save the hospital’s Chinese name.
  • Confirm the exact campus, building, department, and entrance.
  • Confirm whether an appointment is required and whether English support must be arranged.
  • Do not assume that a public international department represents the whole hospital.
  • Keep every receipt, itemized invoice or fapiao, medical record, test report, prescription, and discharge note.
