Questions you can show the restaurant

  • 请问这里是清真餐厅吗？Is this a halal restaurant?
  • 菜里有猪肉、猪油或料酒吗？Does the food contain pork, lard or cooking wine?
  • 厨具和炸油会和其他菜共用吗？Are utensils or frying oil shared with other dishes?
  • 可以把餐厅的中文名和地址发给我吗？Could you send me the restaurant’s Chinese name and address?
EVIDENCEWhat the labels mean

Evidence—not certification.

Labels separate public listings, restaurant statements, and diner reports. Confidence does not mean the kitchen was inspected.

Publicly identified

Public sources use halal or 清真.

Restaurant states halal

The venue describes itself as halal.

Diner reports only

Recent reports exist, but official evidence is limited.

Directory disclaimer

Adapt to China does not inspect or certify restaurants. Ingredients, methods, alcohol service, and pork handling may change. Verify the branch directly.
