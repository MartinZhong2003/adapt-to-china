01. Task-Based SupportUS$20 / US$50 / US$80 by scope
Best when one specific local problem needs more than a generic answer

Start with the situation, not a payment. We first clarify what you need, the city, timing, must-have criteria, and what a useful result looks like. I then confirm whether it is a Light, Standard, or Complex Task. For normal task work, 50% is paid to start and the remaining 50% is due when the agreed deliverable and handoff are completed and confirmed.

LIGHT TASK · US$20

One clear request. Limited moving parts.

For a focused local search, a small number of practical checks, or limited provider contact when the need is already clear.

  • One specific need in one city
  • Usually a quick search/check or contact with up to 2 providers
  • Clear answer, next step, or 1–2 usable options
  • No extended comparison or back-and-forth coordination
STANDARD TASK · US$50

More research, comparison, and one clear next step.

For a bounded task that needs more than a quick lookup: several checks or provider contacts, key details compared, and a small shortlist you can act on.

  • Requirement clarification + targeted local search
  • Check several relevant providers or options
  • Compare the details that matter, such as price, location, availability, language, or fit
  • Usually 2–3 usable options + one follow-up confirmation on the option you prefer
COMPLEX TASK · US$80

Several steps. I source, screen, compare, and coordinate.

For a deeper task with multiple moving parts, wider sourcing, repeated verification, or a more involved handoff before you can act.

  • Detailed requirement clarification + targeted local sourcing
  • Multiple-provider outreach, screening, price / availability comparison, and fit checks
  • A concise comparison deck or structured shortlist when useful
  • Follow-up coordination and a usable handoff to the option you choose
HOW THE SERVICE WORKS

From a vague local problem to a verified next step.

The exact deliverable changes by task, but the working process is usually the same.

1 · UNDERSTAND

We clarify the need first.

A short call or message confirms your city, timing, must-have criteria, previous attempts, and what success should look like.

2 · SCOPE & DEPOSIT

I confirm the level, delivery, and boundary.

You see whether the task is US$20, US$50, or US$80 before work starts. For normal task work, 50% is paid to begin.

3 · SEARCH & VERIFY

I research the local Chinese market.

I search relevant platforms and sources, contact providers when needed, and verify the live details that matter instead of forwarding raw search results.

4 · CURATE

I turn the findings into usable choices.

Depending on the task, you receive a concise written shortlist or comparison deck with practical details such as price, location, photos, contact information, and trade-offs.

5 · COORDINATE

You choose; I help move the next step forward.

When included in scope, I make the follow-up call, confirm the chosen option, arrange a time or contact handoff, and help establish a clear communication channel.

6 · CLOSE

We confirm the handoff, then close the task.

At the agreed closing milestone, you can confirm that the deliverable or handoff is usable. Where appropriate, I allow 1–2 days after the handoff / service use for that check; the remaining balance is then due, and I may invite you to leave a short review.

02. House Finding SupportUS$150 US$75 to start · US$75 after delivery
Help finding a home, comparing your choices, and arranging visits

Looking for a home in China but not sure where to start? I search Chinese rental websites, ask landlords or apartment managers about the details, and explain your options in English. I also help arrange your first visits. I work online; this package does not include coming with you in person.

Download the housing questionnaire (about 12–15 minutes) →

BEFORE I START SEARCHINGUS$75

First, we agree on your needs, what I will do, and the schedule. You then pay US$75 so I can start searching and checking homes.

AFTER THE REPORT, CALL & ARRANGEMENTSUS$75

Pay the remaining US$75 after you receive the report, we finish the video call, and I arrange the agreed viewings or introduce you to the people showing the homes. Payment does not depend on signing a rental contract.

1 · YOUR NEEDS

Tell me what you need in a home.

Fill in the short questionnaire and tell me what matters most. Before visits where you may need to book quickly, check that your phone works and you can pay in a way the landlord or apartment company accepts.

2 · FIND HOMES

I look for homes that fit your needs.

I search Chinese rental websites and ask local contacts about available homes. You do not have to rely only on English-language websites.

3 · CHECK THE DETAILS

I ask landlords and managers the key questions.

Is the home still available? Can a foreign tenant rent it? What are the rent, extra fees, and rental dates? I also ask about the location, furniture, appliances, pets, and your other needs.

4 · COMPARE YOUR OPTIONS

I explain your options in English.

You get an English report comparing the homes. I aim to include 4–6 options, but there may be fewer that fit. One video call of up to 30 minutes is included to explain the choices and viewing plans.

5 · VISIT THE HOMES

You choose; I arrange the viewings.

Choose up to three homes from the report. I arrange your first visits and confirm when, where, and whom to meet, depending on available times. I can also set up a WeChat group if helpful.

6 · AFTER THE VISITS

I help answer your questions after the visits.

Send one set of questions after the visits. I help explain the landlord’s or manager’s replies and discuss your next step. Extra searches, repeated talks about prices or terms, and staying online throughout visits are not included. We agree on any extra work and price first.

Price and payment
US$150 for new bookings. Pay US$75 before the search. Pay US$75 after the report, video call, and agreed viewing arrangements or introductions are complete. You do not need to wait until you sign a rental contract.
What is included
The six steps above: a questionnaire and agreed list of needs, one search, an English report, one video call (up to 30 minutes), arrangements to visit up to three homes, and one set of follow-up questions. I aim for 4–6 homes in the report, depending on availability.
What to tell me first
Where you work or study, your maximum one-way travel time from door to door, your budget, move-in dates, and how long you want to rent. Also tell me if you want your own place or a shared home, how much space and storage you need, required furniture and appliances, pets, allergies, smoke sensitivity, sunlight and noise preferences, and whether you would pay extra viewing or agent fees.
If your needs change
A different area, a new budget, or an extra requirement may mean searching again. Before I do more work, we will agree on what you need next and any extra fee. Sharing a concern does not automatically start a new search.
What you need to check in person
Photos and messages cannot tell the whole story. During the visit, check smells, possible allergy triggers, sunlight, noise, cleanliness, room size, and the condition of the home. Check hallways and other shared areas too.
Getting ready for viewings
For visits where you may need to reserve a home quickly, make sure people can reach you by phone and you can use a payment method the landlord or apartment company accepts. You pay reservation fees, deposits, rent, agent fees, and other housing costs yourself. I cannot lend or pay this money for you.
Costs not included in US$150
Rent, deposits, bills such as water and electricity, platform charges, and fees for agents or people who show you homes are separate. You should be told the amount and asked to agree first. You pay the person or company providing that service directly.
When I reply and how I help
I normally reply within one working day, not immediately or around the clock. Coming with you, translating throughout a viewing, watching every group message, or repeatedly discussing prices or terms with landlords is not included unless we agree separately.
What the fee pays for
You pay for the agreed search, report, call, and arrangements, not a promise that you will rent a home. The second US$75 is due when those steps are complete, even if you decide not to rent. Homes may become unavailable. You choose the home and decide whether to sign.
Things I cannot do for you
I am not your real-estate agent, lawyer, landlord, or property manager. I cannot give legal advice, sign a rental contract, accept its terms for you, log into your accounts, make payments for you, or make decisions that commit you to a deal.

Please remember: I help with the search, basic questions, comparisons, and arrangements we agree on. I do not provide legal advice about contracts, resolve legal disputes, or verify legal ownership. Ask a qualified professional for those checks, and make your own signing decisions.
